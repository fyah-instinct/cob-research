## How the recent planning processes unfolded

Durations below run from the first documented committee/public work to adoption or latest documented milestone. They are not uniform contract-to-adoption measurements. Grant awards are *not* the full project cost. No complete consultant or total project cost could be established from the sources reviewed. A plan describes proposed policies and goals; it does not establish that all proposals have been enacted.

### Town of Lloyd

- **Duration:** Committee work and consultant Laberge Group began in 2022 in two phases; the Smart Growth phase began in 2023, a draft is dated June 2024, and a state adoption notice appeared December 31, 2024. About two years from initial work to the notice; the exact board adoption date was not verified. [Vision Lloyd report](https://www.townoflloyd.com/sites/g/files/vyhlif3371/f/uploads/vision-report-final-235pp-2023-04-13.pdf); [Smart Growth draft](https://www.townoflloyd.com/sites/g/files/vyhlif3371/f/news/lloyd_comprehensive_plan_smart_growth_lr_6-14-2024_0.pdf); [state notice](https://dec.ny.gov/news/environmental-notice-bulletin/2024-12-31/seqr/town-of-lloyd-adoption-of-town-of-lloyd-comprehensive-plan-update).
- **Funding:** The Vision Lloyd phase cites a $10,000 grant; the New York Department of State awarded $41,090 for the Smart Growth phase. These are grants, not a total cost. [Vision Lloyd report](https://www.townoflloyd.com/sites/g/files/vyhlif3371/f/uploads/vision-report-final-235pp-2023-04-13.pdf); [state award list](https://dos.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2022/12/sgcp_2022awards.pdf).
- **Scope:** Broad smart-growth plan building on earlier planning; surveys, workshops, interviews and goals for housing, infrastructure, mobility, climate and land use. [Smart Growth draft](https://www.townoflloyd.com/sites/g/files/vyhlif3371/f/news/lloyd_comprehensive_plan_smart_growth_lr_6-14-2024_0.pdf).

### Town of Fishkill

- **Duration:** An 18-volunteer committee first met September 29, 2021 and met one or two times monthly; survey in spring 2022, open house in October 2022, adoption May 3, 2023. About 19 months from first committee meeting. [Adopted plan](https://www.fishkill-ny.gov/uploads/3/8/0/7/38072219/t_fishkl_cpu_vol_1_plan_text_for_adoption_050323_prnt.pdf).
- **Cost:** No public total or consultant contract price established from the reviewed plan. The plan names technical contributors, but that does not establish their fee. [Adopted plan](https://www.fishkill-ny.gov/uploads/3/8/0/7/38072219/t_fishkl_cpu_vol_1_plan_text_for_adoption_050323_prnt.pdf).
- **Scope:** A substantial update to the 2009 plan, with a point-by-point progress review, reordered priorities, new objectives and environmental and remaining-development analysis; not a clean-slate rewrite. [Adopted plan](https://www.fishkill-ny.gov/uploads/3/8/0/7/38072219/t_fishkl_cpu_vol_1_plan_text_for_adoption_050323_prnt.pdf).

### Town of Philipstown

- **Duration:** Update committee formed November 2017; town-wide surveys ran in 2018 and 2020 with roughly 1,000 combined responses; the plan was adopted November 17, 2021. About four years from committee formation. [Adopted plan, process section](https://cms3.revize.com/revize/philipstown/Documents/Advisory%20Committees/2022-03-30-Philipstown-Comprehesive-Plan-FINAL.pdf).
- **Funding:** $15,000 Hudson River Valley Greenway grant announced in March 2019, not a full project cost. Consultant Nelson, Pope & Voorhis is credited in the plan. [Grant report](https://highlandscurrent.org/2019/03/14/philipstown-receives-15000-grant/); [adopted plan](https://cms3.revize.com/revize/philipstown/Documents/Advisory%20Committees/2022-03-30-Philipstown-Comprehesive-Plan-FINAL.pdf).
- **Scope:** Broad 2030 plan with sustainability as the organizing framework and goals covering housing, climate, transport and land use, including discussion of town-village consolidation. [Adopted plan](https://cms3.revize.com/revize/philipstown/Documents/Advisory%20Committees/2022-03-30-Philipstown-Comprehesive-Plan-FINAL.pdf).

### Town of Poughkeepsie

- **Duration:** Committee active by early 2019, public workshop in July 2020, final draft dated August 4, 2021, and adoption October 6, 2021. More than two and a half years from documented committee work. [Town committee page](https://www.townofpoughkeepsie-ny.gov/252/Comprehensive-Plan-Review-Committee-CPRC); [workshop appendix](https://www.townofpoughkeepsie-ny.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1582/Appendix-B---Workshop-Summaries-PDF?bidId=).
- **Cost:** No full cost or consultant contract price established from these sources. [Town committee page](https://www.townofpoughkeepsie-ny.gov/252/Comprehensive-Plan-Review-Committee-CPRC).
- **Scope:** Extensive update of the 2007 plan with climate-smart priorities and workshops, rather than a narrow text amendment. [Town committee page](https://www.townofpoughkeepsie-ny.gov/252/Comprehensive-Plan-Review-Committee-CPRC); [workshop appendix](https://www.townofpoughkeepsie-ny.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1582/Appendix-B---Workshop-Summaries-PDF?bidId=).

### Town of New Windsor

- **Duration:** Public survey launched August 3, 2020; a working draft was presented to the board February 3, 2021, and the town lists a plan dated April 2021. Roughly eight to nine months from public kickoff to the dated plan. The precise adoption resolution was not verified. [Town announcement](https://nwdnn9.azurewebsites.net/New-Windsor-Connect/Town-Press-Releases/community-survey-for-the-town-of-new-windsor-comprehensive-master-plan); [working draft](https://newwindsor-ny.gov/Portals/0/Content/Comprehensive%20Plan%20Draft%20-%20January%202021.pdf?ver=2021-02-02-151739-627); [town listing](https://www.newwindsor-ny.gov/Home/PageYear1036/2037/PageMonth1036/3).
- **Cost:** Consultant Lanc & Tully is named, but the contract price or full cost was not established. [Working draft](https://newwindsor-ny.gov/Portals/0/Content/Comprehensive%20Plan%20Draft%20-%20January%202021.pdf?ver=2021-02-02-151739-627).
- **Scope:** A nine-chapter update of the 2009 plan, covering land use, housing, infrastructure and resource protection. [Working draft](https://newwindsor-ny.gov/Portals/0/Content/Comprehensive%20Plan%20Draft%20-%20January%202021.pdf?ver=2021-02-02-151739-627).

### Town of Newburgh (update underway)

- **Duration:** Committee review of the 2005 plan's action items documented by February 2023; town posted a draft generic environmental impact statement in January 2026. This is an ongoing process, not a completed adoption clock. [Committee material](https://www.townofnewburghny.gov/documents/Town_of_Newburgh_Comp_Plan_meeting_2-27-23_documents_redux_(2).pdf); [town update page](https://www.townofnewburghny.gov/cn/webpage.cfm?tpid=18630).
- **Cost:** No full cost established in these materials. [Town update page](https://www.townofnewburghny.gov/cn/webpage.cfm?tpid=18630).
- **Scope:** The committee reviewed senior housing, Route 9W zoning, electric-vehicle charging and tree preservation among the plan's action items. The draft GEIS is not an adopted plan. [Committee material](https://www.townofnewburghny.gov/documents/Town_of_Newburgh_Comp_Plan_meeting_2-27-23_documents_redux_(2).pdf); [town update page](https://www.townofnewburghny.gov/cn/webpage.cfm?tpid=18630).

### City of Beacon

- **Duration:** Update effort began around June 2016 and the city adopted its plan April 3, 2017, about ten months. [Contemporaneous report](https://highlandscurrent.org/2016/12/04/beacon-comp-plan-nearly-complete/); [adopted plan](https://beaconny.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Beacon-Comprehensive-Plan-Final.pdf).
- **Cost:** Consultant BFJ is named but a contract price or project total was not established. [Contemporaneous report](https://highlandscurrent.org/2016/12/04/beacon-comp-plan-nearly-complete/).
- **Scope:** Reused public outreach from the 2007 plan, ran two workshops attended by over 100 people each, and focused changes on waterfront/train-station density and height. [Contemporaneous report](https://highlandscurrent.org/2016/12/04/beacon-comp-plan-nearly-complete/); [adopted plan](https://beaconny.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Beacon-Comprehensive-Plan-Final.pdf).

## Reading the table

"Recent", "older" and "very old" are descriptive relative ages, not legal judgments. Unverified adoption dates are flagged in the notes. The table is a research snapshot as of 2026-09-26; check the linked municipality and state sources for later updates.
